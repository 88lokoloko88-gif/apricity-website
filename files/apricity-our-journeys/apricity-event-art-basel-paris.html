How Paris invented modern art

Paris is a different city for everyone, and it leaves no one indifferent. Why did it become the centre of gravity for painters, sculptors and the bohemian world at the start of the last century?

Paris taught the world to look at art differently. For the fifth year running, it is where that conversation happens — and this is six days spent inside it, with the dealers, collectors and rooms that are usually closed.
